# Latopis Nechrubelski

A doom prophecy read one psalm at a time: the reader rolls, a psalm is revealed, and the book fills up until the finale. This glossary fixes the vocabulary the code and the tickets use.

## Language

**Psalm**:
One of the seven prophecies in the book. Psalms I–VI hold six verses each; Psalm VII holds a single verse, numbered 7.

**Verse**:
A numbered line of a psalm, and the unit narration is keyed on: one recording per verse, and the verse being read is the one highlighted.
_Avoid_: line, stanza, section

**Roll**:
The dice throw that reveals the next psalm. It no longer singles out a verse; the only highlighted verse is the one narration is reading.

**Reveal**:
A psalm becoming readable to one reader, as the result of a roll. A psalm stays revealed for the rest of that reading session.

**Reading session**:
One reader's run through the book, from first roll to reset. Holds which psalms have been revealed.

**Variant**:
One of four complete recordings of every verse, differing only in the reader's intonation — sermonic through demonic. Labelled A, B, C and D, and never named in the UI.
_Avoid_: voice, take, version

**Narration**:
The recorded reading of a psalm: one audio file per verse, played in verse order.

**Preview**:
A single randomly chosen narration file, played while the reader is choosing a variant, to demonstrate its intonation. Flavour rather than content, and never the finale (7:7).
